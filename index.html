📝 Prompt Lab

Tap Copy, paste into Tab 2 or 3 (or your Google Flow app). Fill in the [brackets].

📋 Cheat sheet — good video prompts in 5 pieces:
1. Subject — who/what is in frame ("a stray dog", "an old fisherman")
2. Action — ONE clear thing happening ("shakes off water in slow motion")
3. Camera — how it moves ("slow push-in", "static tripod", "drone rising")
4. Light — ("golden hour", "moonlight", "neon glow")
5. Mood — ("peaceful", "eerie", "joyful")

⚠️ One idea per clip. Short clips beat long wish-lists. If it fails, simplify the action first, not the adjectives.
